Experiments

Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction

The Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction is a striking example of a chemical system that does not settle to equilibrium, but instead exhibits sustained oscillations and spontaneous pattern formation, such as rotating spirals and travelling waves. This behaviour arises from a complex interplay of nonlinear reaction kinetics and diffusion, making it a prototypical example of a reaction–diffusion system.

As a closure of my PhD research, I have decided to perform these delicate chemical reactions myself. Click here for my repository on these experiments, where you can find more information and photos. Click here for my YouTube channel.
